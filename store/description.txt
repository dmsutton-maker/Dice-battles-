Two dice. Six colours. Six prisoners to set free.

Roll both dice. Land the SAME colour on both and that prisoner runs free. Land two different colours and nothing happens — so roll again.

That is the whole game, and a five-year-old gets it on the first turn.

Then it becomes a race. Somebody is rolling against you, emptying their own jail, and you have to free all six before they do.


NOTHING TO READ

The dice show colours, not numbers or pips. Nothing to count, nothing to squint at, nothing to read. That is why it works for someone who cannot read yet and someone who would rather not reach for their glasses — at the same table, in the same game.


FOUR WAYS TO PLAY

• Color Rush — free all six. The one to learn on.
• Ultimate — roll a colour you have already freed and they go BACK to jail.
• Skirmish — one shared jail, so you are taking prisoners off each other.
• Color War — you each get one colour, and race to free your three.


TWO PLAYERS, ONE PHONE

Split screen, both halves live at once. Nothing to pass back and forth.


THROW THEM PROPERLY

The dice are real physics, not an animation. Tap to roll, or swipe to throw them the way you swiped — harder for a bigger throw. And you never have to wait: swipe again and the next roll is already on its way.


EARN YOUR WAY UP

Win battles, earn trophies, climb the ladder. Unlock new battlefields — a castle at sunset, a jungle clearing, a station in orbit — and dice in wood, marble, granite, gold and silver.


BUILT SO NOBODY IS LEFT OUT

Colourblind mode stamps every colour with its own shape, so a match is a match whatever you see. Easy, Medium and Hard opponents, so the person who has played a hundred games and the person on their first can both find a fair fight.


NO ACCOUNT, NO LOGIN

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to remember. Works on a plane.
